# Production-state campaign and replies

Use the single offer and qualification contract in [ASSESSMENT.md](ASSESSMENT.md). Internal overlapping pain tags support analysis; prospects see one diagnostic and one offer.

## Three-touch causal progression

**Touch 1: transferability.** Could another creator who didn't build the original AI workflow reproduce or extend approved work tomorrow without needing the original creator?

**Touch 2: missing production state.** Some execution state may live in the workflow while approved references, source versions, client decisions, canonical outputs, and human production context remain outside it. Ask which required state must still be reconstructed or supplied manually.

**Touch 3: economic consequence.** Generation capacity is rising faster than coordination capacity. If senior operators remain context routers, increasing AI output may produce rework and lower margins instead of proportional production capacity. Ground this in the prospect's own examples rather than claiming universal savings.

**Offer:** Make one live AI production team-operable: preserve its approved production state, keep creators on the correct inputs, and make approved work reproducible and transferable—without replacing the tools the team already uses.

Link to `/assessment` using existing validated identity prefill and UTM attribution. Explain that the diagnostic identifies the production capability worth fixing first and does not commit the prospect to a pilot or meeting.

## Responding to evidence

Read the full reply. Preserve meaningful customer language in the existing Apollo activity or contact Note; do not duplicate subjective interpretations into custom fields.

- A problem confirmation identifies an actual dependence, missing state, incorrect input, or production consequence. Interest in AI tooling alone is not a problem confirmation.
- An active relevant workflow identifies current, imminent, or recurring production. Hypothetical experiments alone do not qualify for immediate deployment.
- A mature production pipeline can still lack generative dependencies or approved operational context. Ask what is required to reproduce approved work; do not describe the pipeline as primitive or broken.
- Reliable handoff can coexist with wrong-reference failures, high-volume variant problems, R&D reconstruction, or non-deterministic agent context. Ask about the relevant production failure instead of insisting on originator dependence.
- Bring provenance into the conversation when controlled IP, required traceability, or source exposure makes it relevant.
