Direct Production Team onboarding

You are correct to charge for onboarding if it is materially expensive.

Required one-time onboarding fees are an accepted model when implementation requires configuration, migration, or training; for example, HubSpot currently discloses required one-time onboarding charges of $1,500 or $3,000 on several professional products.

Validate the fee with a cost formula
Required onboarding price =
fully loaded delivery cost
÷ (1 − target services gross margin)

Example:

12 hours × $125 = $1,500 delivery cost

At a 40% services gross margin:
$1,500 ÷ 0.60 = $2,500 price

That supports the proposed $2,500 fee.

If the actual onboarding consumes 20 hours:

20 × $125 = $2,500 cost
$2,500 ÷ 0.60 = $4,167 required price

In that case, either:

charge more;
reduce scope;
automate onboarding;
move the customer into the Studio implementation path.

The fee should be driven by actual delivery economics, not merely by what looks acceptable beside the subscription.
